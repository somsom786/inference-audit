RTX 4070 12 GB + 64 GB DDR5 experiment

Place the looped core on the GPU.

Inkling-2-Mini (16.6 GB) is larger than a 12 GB GPU, so some experts must stay in host RAM. Moving its 3×-looped core blocks into VRAM—instead of letting llama.cpp auto-fit choose—delivered ~37% faster decode, ~55% faster 8K prefill, and ~68% faster startup on the exact same card.

The architecture insight

Why loop-aware placement wins.

Inkling-2-Mini has 24 physical transformer blocks that execute as 48 logical layers. Blocks blk.6 through blk.17 form a looped core that executes three times per generated token (36 logical passes).

Those 12 core blocks take up only 6.88 GB of the model's 14.78 GB of routed experts, yet they generate 72.3% of all active expert traffic per token (1,289.9 of 1,783.6 MB). Every gigabyte of VRAM spent on the looped core eliminates three times as much host-RAM work as a gigabyte spent on the once-executed prelude or postlude.

On this 12 GB RTX 4070 (431 GB/s measured copy bandwidth), 11 of the 12 core blocks (blk.6..16) plus all 1.82 GB of non-expert weights and the 16,384-token f16 KV cache fit on CUDA0. The prelude, blk.17, and postlude experts execute on the CPU from pinned host DDR5 RAM (--load-mode none), avoiding mmap page-fault overhead and cutting startup from ~12 s to ~3.7 s.

Versus --cpu-moe

2.1×

Decode 2.1× faster (+108–110%); 8K cold prefill 8.1–8.2× faster (796–799 vs 97–98 tok/s).

Honest hardware boundary: Putting all 12 core blocks on the GPU reached 37.00 tok/s, but drove board VRAM to 11,057 MiB (leaving only 954 MiB free alongside Windows desktop apps) and tripped our safety guard. Keeping 11 core blocks on CUDA trades ~2.5 tok/s for 1,333–1,468 MiB of safe desktop headroom.

Reproducibility & limits

Exact launch flags and shareable summary.

Winning configuration loop-core-11-nommap-ub512 pins prelude (blk.0..5), blk.17, and postlude (blk.18..23) routed experts to host RAM while keeping all non-expert layers and 11 looped core blocks on CUDA0.

llama-server -m <MODEL_GGUF> --jinja --no-mmproj -c 16384 -np 1 --reasoning-effort low --fit off -ngl all \
  -ot "blk\.([0-5]|1[7-9]|2[0-3])\.ffn_.*_exps\.=CPU" \
  --load-mode none -fa on -b 512 -ub 512 -t 8 -tb 8

Model: Unsloth Inkling-2-Mini UD-Q4_K_M GGUF (16,610,543,328 bytes, 0 embedded DSpark tensors). Hardware: RTX 4070 12 GB, Ryzen 7 7800X3D, 64 GB DDR5-6000. Engine: Unsloth llama.cpp b12588.

Scope & unverified areas: One machine, one model, single stream, 16K context, lossless f16 KV, text only (--no-mmproj), Windows 11. Not an officially accepted local-ai-registry recipe. Vision, broader output-quality benchmarks, multi-user production concurrency, external customer infrastructure, and financial savings remain unverified.
