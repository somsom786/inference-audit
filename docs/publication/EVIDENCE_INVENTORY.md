The public figures can be regenerated using only the [numeric projection](../../public/evidence/measurements.json). Full response bodies, exact traffic fixtures, profiling databases, logs, and GPU host identity remain in local evidence. A fresh analysis of all original observations requires the local archives and access to the relevant data. Independent service validation on another machine, uninterrupted three-hour endurance and native CUDA shutdown are outstanding. Deployment, model rights and public hosting approvals are separate from numerical verification.

The chart and report SVGs are generated by this project. The landing page uses operating-system fonts and local assets. No weights, engine binaries, font binaries or third-party artwork are distributed with the public proof package. The toolkit uses the repository's [MIT license](../../LICENSE). Models and llama.cpp are separately obtained under their respective terms. Benchmark references identify software and model variants.
