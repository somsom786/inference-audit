For operators and finance teams

What could more capacity be worth?

For a fixed amount of useful work, a 30% gain in qualified throughput could use 23.1% fewer GPU hours. If your bill is fixed, the same gain can give you headroom while cash savings remain zero.

Qualified throughput means correct tasks finished within your latency and context requirements. Use the improvement measured on your workload.
